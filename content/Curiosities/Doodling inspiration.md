As I've been learning to make a habit of drawing and sketching lately, I've chanced upon a website that's great for this purpose, [Wiki Spy](https://neal.fun/wiki-spy/)  by Neal.fun. 
![[Screenshot 2026-09-11 at 5.17.10 PM.png]]
It randomly pulls images from Wikipedia which helps me when I have no idea what to draw. You can click on an image to find related subjects as well! Have fun.
