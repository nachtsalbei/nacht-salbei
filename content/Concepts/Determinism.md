## Definition
Determinism is the philosophical and scientific idea that every event, action, and choice is the inevitable result of preceding events and the laws of nature. It assumes that if an observer has sufficient information about an object or human being, then such an observer might be able to predict every consequent move of that object or human being.

In a hypothetical example, lawyers in a deterministic world may argue that an offender, say he stole some bread to feed his family, is wholly innocent, for his act of stealing has been preordained due to circumstance. It was not his fault that he had fallen onto bad times, that someone drove onto incoming traffic and collided into him, rendering him unable to work, that he was born into poverty and did not get a chance to pursue higher education, that his parents squandered their inheritance.

This results in an unbroken chain of events that can stretch back to the origin of the universe.

## Thoughts
Do we live in a deterministic world? Are all events preordained? In terms of science, at least in the classical physical world, we work on the assumptions that given a set of parameters, it is possible to calculate the future outcome. That forms the basis of our science currently.
