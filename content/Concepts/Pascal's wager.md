[Blaise Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaise_Pascal) (1623-1662) argues that people should believe in God after carefully weighing both potential outcomes on whether or not God exists. 

If, by adopting a lifestyle that is consistent with religious doctrine (*i.e. to sacrifice some daily comforts and luxuries, living modestly and piously*), and God turns out to be real, then the believer stands to gain an infinite upside in the form of paradise, eternal life, while avoiding damnation in Hell. They traded finite losses for infinite value. Even if God is not real, then the losses are still finite.

On the other hand, if a person chooses to sin and live a hedonic lifestyle, forsaking God, and God turns out to be real, then they are condemned to eternal suffering. Their finite comforts have led to infinite damnation.
